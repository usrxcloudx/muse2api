Muse2API Cookie Importer —— Huong dan cai dat
===================================================

Tien ich nay chi lam mot viec duy nhat: Dong bo Cookie dang nhap muse.ai
tu trinh duyet cua ban vao dich vu muse2api. Khong can cai Python, khong can mo terminal.


I. Giai nen
-----------
Giai nen muse2api-extension.zip vao thu muc co dinh tren may, vi du:

    Windows : D:\muse2api-extension
    macOS   : ~/Documents/muse2api-extension
    Linux   : ~/workspace/ai-world/muse2api/extension

Sau khi giai nen se co cac tep: manifest.json, popup.html, popup.js.


II. Cai dat vao Chrome / Edge
-----------------------------
Google Chrome:
  1. Nhap  chrome://extensions  vao thanh dia chi roi nhan Enter
  2. Bat cong tac «Che do cho nha phat trien» o goc tren ben phai
  3. Nhan vao «Tai tien ich da giai nen» o goc tren ben trai
  4. Chon thu muc extension da giai nen
  5. Ghim tien ich ra thanh cong cu de tien su dung

Microsoft Edge:
  1. Nhap  edge://extensions  vao thanh dia chi roi nhan Enter
  2. Bat cong tac «Che do nha phat trien» o goc duoi ben trai
  3. Nhan «Tai phan mo rong chua dong goi»
  4. Cac buoc tiep theo tuong tu nhu Chrome


III. Cach su dung
-----------------
1. Tren trinh duyet nay, mo https://muse.ai/ va dang nhap tai khoan (den giao dien chat https://muse.ai/thread/new).
2. Vao trang quan tri muse2api (http://localhost:18610/admin), sao chep BASE URL va API Key.
3. Nhan vao bieu tuong tien ich tren thanh cong cu, dien 2 thong tin tren vao.
4. Nhan nut «Doc va Nhap Cookie».


IV. Cau hoi thuong gap
----------------------
Q: Bao loi «Khong tim thay Cookie nao cua muse.ai»
A: Trinh duyet chua dang nhap tai khoan muse.ai. Hay mo https://muse.ai/ va dang nhap truoc.

Q: Bao loi «Thieu muc cot loi hatch_vml...»
A: Chua mo toi giao dien chat. Hay vao https://muse.ai/thread/new, gui thu 1 cau chat roi bam nhap lai.

Q: Bao loi «API Key khong chinh xac (Ma loi 401)»
A: Vao trang quan tri sao chep lai API Key (bat dau bang m2a_...).
